# Founding Engineer Hiring Narrative Pack

Internal draft for leadership and warm outreach. This document is intentionally narrative-forward and deliberately avoids technical/process details reserved for CTO approval.

## Title and Framing Variants

1. Founding Engineer, Decision Systems Platform
2. Founding Engineer, Symbolic-Computational Product
3. Founding Engineer, Self-Consciousness Infrastructure

## Short Company Narrative for Candidates

Tryambakam Noesis is building a different category of product: a self-consciousness practice platform, not a wellness app, not a therapy proxy, and not a behavior-optimization loop.

Our core product is a decision-mirror system that helps practitioners witness patterns and author meaning in their own lives. We operate across software, narrative systems, and practice protocols, with one non-negotiable principle: the system should increase practitioner independence, not dependency.

We are looking for an engineer who wants to build serious product in a category that does not fit inherited templates.

## Why This Role Matters Now

The company is moving from founder-led velocity into repeatable product execution. The first engineering hire is the leverage point for that shift.

This role matters now because we need a technical partner who can turn a high-context, multi-surface product system into reliable shipping rhythm and durable technical clarity. The work is not about polishing a single feature. It is about helping define how this company builds.

## Candidate FAQ (Draft)

- **What kind of company is this?**
  Tryambakam Noesis is a self-consciousness development company. We build decision systems for pattern witnessing and self-authored meaning.

- **Is this a wellness or therapy product?**
  No. The product framing is practice and discernment, not wellness consumption or therapeutic dependency.

- **What will the founding engineer own?**
  Core ownership is helping establish technical execution discipline, shipping reliability, and product-level engineering decisions across the active platform surfaces.

- **What kind of environment should this person expect?**
  High context, high agency, and direct collaboration with leadership. Clear thinking and execution quality matter more than theater.

- **Is this role narrowly scoped or broad?**
  Broad. This is a foundational role with meaningful influence on how engineering is shaped as a function.

- **Who does this role work with day-to-day?**
  Directly with founder leadership and in close coordination with research, design, and content stakeholders.

- **What kind of candidate usually fits?**
  Someone who can reason from first principles, handle ambiguity without chaos, and ship with rigor.

- **What should candidates evaluate before opting in?**
  Whether they want to help define an engineering function from first principles in a non-standard product category.
