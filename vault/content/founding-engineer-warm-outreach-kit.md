# Founding Engineer Warm Outreach Kit

Internal use only. This is for warm, exploratory outreach while CTO approval for [THO-1](/THO/issues/THO-1) is still pending.

## Framing Guardrails

### Approved Primary Framing
`Founding Engineer, Decision Systems Platform`

Use when:
- first message with limited context
- candidate has strong product/engineering judgment and systems depth
- you want clarity without over-explaining the category

Do not use when:
- the conversation is already deep in symbolic language and category nuance

### Approved Secondary Framing
`Founding Engineer, Symbolic-Computational Product`

Use when:
- the candidate is technical and asks what makes the product category different
- you need a sharper phrase than generic SaaS language

Do not use when:
- the contact is low-context and may read "symbolic-computational" as vague or academic

### Reserved Framing (High Context Only)
`Founding Engineer, Self-Consciousness Infrastructure`

Use when:
- the candidate already understands the practice frame
- the conversation is high-trust and context-rich

Do not use when:
- first-touch outreach
- low-context warm intros
- any message that could be forwarded without explanation

## Safe Claims / Do Not Claim Yet

### Safe to Claim Now
- We are hiring the first engineer.
- Current framing: **Founding Engineer, Decision Systems Platform**.
- Warm exploratory conversations are open now.
- Role expectation is broad ownership and direct collaboration.
- Product category framing: self-consciousness practice platform (not wellness/therapy).

### Do Not Claim Yet (Pending CTO Workstream on [THO-1](/THO/issues/THO-1))
- specific tech stack or architecture boundaries
- compensation/equity ranges
- title leveling or seniority promises
- interview loop or evaluation rubric
- process stages, timeline, or decision owners
- location policy or working model specifics
- 30/60/90 expectations

## Candidate Calibration (Role Is / Is Not)

### This Role Is
- first engineering seat with real surface-area ownership
- high-context and cross-functional (product, research, design, content)
- for builders who can create execution clarity in ambiguous terrain
- for people who value precision, agency, and shipping discipline

### This Role Is Not
- a narrowly scoped implementation lane
- a role defined by pre-set enterprise process
- a public job posting flow with fixed process promises (yet)
- a fit for candidates who need fully pre-structured environments from day one
